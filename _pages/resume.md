## Research

### Independent Research

**[Real-Time Reliability of First-Release Nonfarm Payroll Estimates: Short- and Long-Horizon Evidence from CES Revisions, 2000–2024](https://github.com/Daniel-Elmore/Nonfarm-Payroll-Revisions)**  
*2026 | Independent Research Report, 97 pages*

- Constructed a vintage-consistent dataset of 300 monthly payroll observations spanning 2000–2024 to investigate the reliability of first-release U.S. employment estimates.
- Examined short- and long-horizon revisions, identifying substantial tail risk even outside COVID, including a 95th-percentile absolute long-horizon revision of approximately 201,000 jobs.
- Applied interrupted time series analysis with HAC-robust inference, placebo break tests, and a negative control, estimating a 168,000-job shift in revision magnitude and a 39-percentage-point increase in tail-event probability at the March 2020 structural break.
- Developed quantile gradient boosting models using predictors available at first release, with leakage audits and rolling out-of-sample validation. Found that forecasting performance deteriorated around structural breaks.

**[Unlocking Labor Force Potential Through Fiscal Policy](/projects/undergrad-thesis/)**
